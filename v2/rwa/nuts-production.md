# 🌰 US Nut Economy — RWA

**Status: LIVE** — [orchard.basednut.com/rwa/nuts-production](https://orchard.basednut.com/rwa/nuts-production)

America runs on nuts. From California almonds to Georgia peanuts, U.S. nut farming is a multi-billion-dollar network of regional economies. The kernel is only the start: hulls, shells, skins, oils, meals and orchard wood also have value.

All values are 2025 USDA farm-production estimates. Gray on the map means no current estimate in the series used here, not zero production.

## Seven Crops, Five Americas

A nut carries its geography with it: climate, water, soil, labor, pollinators, processors and trade, compressed into one crop.

| Crop | Region | Scale | Coproducts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Almonds | California · Central Valley | 2.4B lb · $2.46/lb · 1.38M acres | Hulls → dairy feed; shells → mulch, bedding, energy; wood → soil or biochar |
| Pistachios | California · Central Valley | 1.1B lb · $2.82/lb · 520K acres | Hulls → feed; shells → biochar, abrasives, construction fill |
| Walnuts | California · Central Valley | 720M lb · $1.53/lb · 385K acres | Shells → abrasives, filtration media, biochar; husks → compost |
| Peanuts | Georgia + Southeast · Coastal Plain | 6.2B lb · $0.24/lb · 1.6M acres | Shells → biomass; skins → ingredients; crushing → oil + protein meal |
| Pecans | New Mexico + Southwest · River valleys | 290M lb · $1.93/lb · 210K acres | Shells, husks, wood → mulch, compost, biochar, energy, biomaterials |
| Hazelnuts | Oregon · Willamette Valley | 110M lb · $1.18/lb · 85K acres | Shells → long-lived mulch, heat and biochar research |
| Macadamias | Hawaii · Tropical orchards | 30.6M lb · $1.04/lb · 16.3K acres | Shells → fuel or mulch; husks → compost; culled kernels → oil; press cake → feed |

## Beyond the Kernel — Six Coproduct Streams

What leaves the factory besides food? Six high-value coproduct streams, each labeled by evidence stage:

| Coproduct stream | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Hulls → dairy feed | A large existing dairy-feed market. UC ANR estimates roughly $160M–$230M/year in hull value. **In use** |
| Shells → mulch, bedding, energy | Almond shells have established bedding uses; UC is evaluating direct orchard return as a potassium amendment and mulch. **In use** |
| Shells → fuel | Real in Hawaii: Mauna Loa has used macadamia shells and waste as biofuel at Keaʻau. **In use** |
| Skins → ingredients | USDA estimates 40M–70M lb of peanut skins removed each year, developing food/feed uses around nutrients and bioactives. **Developing** |
| Kernels → oil, flour + meal | Established in peanuts and hazelnuts, plus specialty macadamia oil. Pecan oil/flour tested at pilot scale. **Established / pilot** |
| Shell carbon → advanced materials | USDA is developing shell-derived activated carbon and, in a 2026 project, peanut-shell biographite for lithium-battery anodes. **R&D** |

## 🧾 Evidence Rule

"**In use**" means a direct source documents an operating use. "**Industrial product**" means the item is commercially offered or manufactured. "**R&D / pilot**" means the pathway is being tested and should not be counted as an established market. Primary evidence is linked inline throughout the source page.

## Regional Stories

- **California · Central Valley** — dry Central Valley supports vast almond, pistachio and walnut orchards through managed water, pollination and dense processing infrastructure.
- **Oregon · Willamette Valley** — Oregon's wetter Willamette Valley is the center of U.S. hazelnut production, rebuilt around disease-resistant cultivars.
- **Georgia + Southeast · Coastal Plain** — peanuts grow underground in annual rotations across Georgia and the Southeast, tied to shellers, crushers and food manufacturers.
- **New Mexico + Southwest** — pecans follow irrigation through river valleys, creating long-lived green orchards in hot, arid valleys.
- **Hawaii · Tropical orchards** — macadamia orchards pair oil-rich kernels with unusually hard shells, making local reuse especially valuable.
