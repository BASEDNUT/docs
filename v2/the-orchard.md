### 🥜 What is a NUT?

> **NUT = Network Utility Token**

A **NUT** is not just our brand. It can also be an acronym that stands for **Network Utility Token**—a generalized, composable representation of an tokenized asset or position in a network.

<figure><img src="/files/lqUmvL21MnGWzUdDPeha" alt="" width="188"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

In our platform we use $NUT as the main "Network Utility Token" as the main liquidity seed for the entire ecosystem.

NUTs are the **living seeds of liquidity**—rooted in soil, structured by smart contracts, harvested by users and protocols alike.

NUTs are the building blocks across The Orchard system.

#### Saplings and Nut Trees

<figure><img src="/files/E3sIDR3ydyn9kM2o6gEq" alt="" width="188"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Saplings and Nut Trees are another way of describing liquidity pools in The Orchard ecosystem. The process is simple:

1. Combine a Soil Mix with a Nut Seed.
2. Plant the Nut Seed to grow a liquidity tree.
3. Depending on its maturity, the tree can be a **Sapling** or a fully grown **Nut Tree**.

**Saplings**

<figure><img src="/files/sRhldbFF4PkPRqVRU8E3" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Saplings represent a transformative leap in decentralized finance, serving as the linchpin of liquidity provisioning, token innovation, and ecosystem development. These **ERC-20 tokens** encapsulate the logic of liquidity pool (LP) tokens while adding advanced programmability, making them versatile instruments for optimizing liquidity, governance, and dynamic market participation.

**Key Features of Saplings:**

* **Evolving Financial Instruments**: Saplings can evolve into synthetic assets, yield-bearing tokens, programmable governance units, and dynamic arbitrage tools.
* **Liquidity Frameworks**: They provide robust liquidity frameworks and enhance user engagement.
* **Sophisticated Tokenomics**: Redefine liquidity management and create new opportunities for decentralized finance participants.

**Nut Trees**

Nut Trees are the mature stage of liquidity pools, offering advanced features and dynamic market adaptability. They represent the pinnacle of liquidity evolution, combining programmability and adaptability to stabilize liquidity and drive growth.
