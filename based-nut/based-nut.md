**BASED NUT** is a playful **DeFi experiment** that combines the fun of memes with DeFi functionality.

It creates a unique ecosystem that intertwines humor with experimental utility, focusing on providing a whimsical yet dynamic experience.

***

The **BASED NUT** ecosystem is centered around three core tokens: **$NUT, $SNUT, & $pNUT**, which work together to create layers of liquidity and interactions along with the rest of the peripheral tokens.
