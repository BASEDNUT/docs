# NUT

## BASED NUT ($NUT)

### Overview

The **Network Utility Token ($NUT)** serves as the foundational layer in the BASED NUT ecosystem. It is designed to connect all other token layers and liquidity interactions within the ecosystem. $NUT is unique due to its extreme scarcity, with a total supply of **just one token**, making it highly exclusive and central to the functionality of the broader ecosystem.

<figure><img src="/files/74mkiQTji9HPEmgVeBld" alt="" width="160"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Key Features

* **No Tax**: There are no taxes or fees associated with $NUT transactions, ensuring efficiency in usage.
* **No Team Allocation**: $NUT was launched with no allocation set aside for developers or the team, making it a fully decentralized token from inception.
* **0 ETH Stealth Launch on Uniswap V3**: $NUT was launched in a 100% NUT concentrated liquidity position with 0 ETH paired.

This makes $NUT the root of a larger, complex token ecosystem, connecting all other tokens and protocols. For more in-depth details and technical explanation of how $NUT integrates with other tokens, refer to the full technical paper.
